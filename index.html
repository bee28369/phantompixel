How PhantomPixel Works

No confusing tech talk. Here is what happens when you protect a file:

👀

1. People see your clear picture

Your photo or PDF keeps 100% of its sharpness and size (even 4K). Friends and clients see no difference at all.

🤖

2. AI gets confused

We change tiny color dots by a fraction too small for human eyes to see, plus scramble hidden PDF text. When AI tries to read it, it gets lost.

🕵️

3. Your secret name stays inside

Your name is woven right into the file itself. If anyone takes your work, drop the file into Check File to prove it belongs to you.

Install on iPhone & Android

PhantomPixel is built for both iOS and Android:

🍎 iPhone / iPad (iOS)
  1. Open this app in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" — it launches full-screen just like an App Store app!
🤖 Android Phone
  1. Open this app in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) and tap "Install App".
  3. It installs right onto your app drawer and works offline!
